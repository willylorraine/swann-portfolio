Flip the funnel. SIBUR, Vivilen

SIBUR built a plant that turns recycled plastic into packaging polymer. It ran at 20%: only 17 of 100 packaging makers would even discuss eco-materials. Makers don't buy slides. They buy demand on the shelf.

Strategy
Flip the funnel. First build measurable demand with shoppers, then take the numbers to manufacturers
Idea
ENTERCYCLE: not an ad, a movement. Enter the recycling loop
Creative
A voting site for eco-packaging, blogger challenges, car-share cars that swapped bottles for ride credits, interactive billboards, and our own marathon bin that catches 30% more bottles
Result
Awareness 8.1% → 15.2%, +13.5 p.p. ready to pay more, 64 qualified leads, plant load 20% → 80%. In the book “50 Cases That Changed Marketing”
RoleIlia Lopukhov, Executive Creative Director, Realweb

A planet unfit for life. Lighthouse

Lighthouse is a children's hospice; 85% of its budget is private donations. People can't picture life with an incurable illness, so they don't give. We made them feel it first.

Idea
Earth itself is a planet unfit for life for thousands of seriously ill children: gravity you can't move against, air you can't breathe
Film
A hand-made animated film about the world through the patients' eyes. Made with Zebra Hero and Invisible Friends, voiced by Alexander Petrov
Special project
An interactive site: hover over a hero to read a real child's story. Every donation lights a star with the donor's name
Media
Actors and bloggers shared it for free; 84 media stories. OOH and media facades in Moscow
Result
4.8M reached, 32K on the site, donations $100K a month. Red Apple Grand Prix, ADCR and Mixx gold, 15+ awards
RoleIlia Lopukhov, Executive Creative Director, Realweb

Show the real you. TECNO × MIXIT

TECNO was launching a camera phone with a 40M reach target, in a market where every launch shouts megapixels. 44% of young women would drop filters if they liked their own face more.

Strategy
No spec war: go to the beauty audience through a partner that shares the value
Idea
Show the real you: a camera to express yourself, skincare to love your real face, no filter
Creative
A two-level box with MIXIT: the phone and earbuds on top, a five-step skincare routine below. Masha Way as the face of the campaign, an Ozon special project, pop-up zones in malls, creator unboxings
Result
80M reached, twice the target. 240K landing visits, 628 boxes sold in three weeks, every KPI beaten 2×
RoleIlia Lopukhov, Chief Creative Officer, Realweb

Names, marks, a typeface and dice. The show identity

Everything around the animation I designed and built with AI: two names, the season and channel marks, a custom typeface and six 3D dice. The AI made; I set the task, chose and finished. For this portfolio the marks and the typeface now exist in English too.

3D first, then AI. One minute of battle

71 seconds of battle from episode four. I stage the scene in Blender, like previs on a feature film. The AI paints over it; the director makes the decisions.

How it's made: from character sheet to a finished shot

Character. Every hero has a sheet: portrait, full body, turnaround, emotions, props. Style and base in Midjourney, refined in Seedream 5 Pro. The same rules keep the family, the house and the product consistent across hundreds of assets.

Animation. Episode breakdown, location plates and start frames with my own skills, then Seedance 2.5.

One episode, a week of content. Reels and cards

Every episode becomes vertical reels and carousel cards built from the same scenes. For this portfolio two reels are fully dubbed into English: transcribed, translated, re-voiced with a cast of AI voices and mixed over the original music and table sound. The cards are re-rendered in English from the same project files.
